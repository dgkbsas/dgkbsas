### Hi, I'm Diego

**Industrial Designer** — obsessed with designing *and* building products, tangible and intangible, around usability and experience.

That's the throughline: I don't stop at the spec. I sit close to the user — early ideation, discovery, mapping real flows — and carry it all the way into the working product. *Less handoff, more iteration.*

---

Currently **Product Builder @ [Wúru](https://www.wuru.ai)** — design + frontend + product definition for healthcare operations software.

- Platform serving **~1.8K daily operational users** across **16 client organizations** and **30+ sites**, with 16 permission roles
- Designing the **human side of AI in production** — an agentic scheduling coworker (how it talks, when it interrupts, how it earns trust), ML duration predictions inside the scheduling UI, conversational data capture
- Taught design at **Universidad de Buenos Aires for 12 years** — shaped how I run critiques and raise the bar on a team
